## About Me

I'm **Oliver** (Gideon Kipkirui), a **full-stack developer and AI engineer** based in Kenya. I design, build, and ship end-to-end products through **[Omix Systems](https://omixsystems.store)** — from trading SaaS and fintech to edtech, marketplaces, real-time apps, and Web3.

I work primarily across **TypeScript, Python, and Rust**, and I'm comfortable moving between frontend, backend, mobile, and desktop (Tauri). I care about turning ideas into real, working software — fast and with care.

> *"Ship the thing. Then make it better."*
